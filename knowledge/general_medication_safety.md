# Medication Safety for Pets

## Consistent dosing times
Give medications at the same time every day. Medications prescribed twice daily should be about 12 hours apart. Mark daily medications as required so they are never dropped from the schedule.

## Medication with food
Many pet medications are better tolerated with food. When a medication must be given with a meal, schedule it right after feeding.

## Never improvise doses
Do not double up on a missed dose or give human medications to pets. Ask a vet what to do if a dose is missed.
