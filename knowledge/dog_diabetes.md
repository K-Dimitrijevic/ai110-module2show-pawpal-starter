# Diabetes in Dogs

## Insulin and meal timing
Diabetic dogs are usually given insulin twice a day, about 12 hours apart, right after a meal so the dog has eaten before the dose. Insulin doses should be treated as required tasks and never skipped or doubled without vet advice.

## Consistent meals
Feed the same amount of the same food at the same times every day. Avoid unplanned treats.

## Exercise consistency
Keep daily exercise similar in length and intensity each day. Sudden intense exercise can drop blood sugar too low.
