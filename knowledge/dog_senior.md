# Senior Dog Care and Arthritis

## Exercise for senior dogs
Dogs are usually considered senior from about 8 years old, earlier for large breeds. Replace one long walk with two or three shorter, gentle walks of 15 to 20 minutes. Avoid long hikes, jumping, and stairs if the dog is stiff.

## Arthritis and joint care
Dogs with arthritis or joint stiffness benefit from consistent low-impact movement every day rather than occasional long outings. A vet-approved joint supplement is often given daily with a meal. Gentle range-of-motion stretching for 5 to 10 minutes can help.

## Senior feeding
Senior dogs often need fewer calories and may do better on two smaller meals. Keep them lean, because extra weight worsens joint pain.

## Health checks
Senior dogs should see a vet every 6 months. Watch for changes in thirst, appetite, mobility, or behavior.
