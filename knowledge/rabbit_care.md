# Rabbit Care

## Rabbit diet
Rabbits need unlimited fresh hay, which should make up most of their diet, plus a daily portion of leafy greens and a small amount of pellets. Refill hay and water every day.

## Exercise for rabbits
Rabbits need at least 3 to 4 hours a day outside their enclosure in a safe, rabbit-proofed area to run and explore.

## Cleaning
Spot-clean the litter area daily and do a full enclosure clean weekly.

## Grooming rabbits
Brush rabbits weekly, and daily during shedding season, because rabbits cannot vomit up swallowed fur.
