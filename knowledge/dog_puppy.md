# Puppy Care

## Exercise limits for puppies
Puppies should not do long or high-impact exercise while their joints are still growing. A common rule of thumb is about 5 minutes of structured walking per month of age, up to twice a day, plus free play. Avoid long hikes and running on hard surfaces.

## Feeding puppies
Puppies under 6 months usually eat three to four small meals a day. Move to two meals a day between 6 and 12 months. Use a food formulated for growth.

## Potty breaks
Young puppies need a potty break after waking, after eating, after play, and roughly every 2 hours during the day. A short 5 minute potty break task helps house training.

## Socialization and training
Short daily training sessions of 5 to 10 minutes work best for puppies. Gentle exposure to new people, sounds, and surfaces before 16 weeks of age helps prevent fear later.
