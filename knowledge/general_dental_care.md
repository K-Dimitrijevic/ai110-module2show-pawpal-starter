# Dental Care for Dogs and Cats

## Tooth brushing
Daily tooth brushing with a pet toothpaste is the most effective way to prevent dental disease in dogs and cats. Even a 2 to 5 minute session a few times a week helps.

## Dental chews
Vet-approved dental chews can reduce plaque in dogs but do not replace brushing.

## Signs of dental problems
Bad breath, drooling, dropping food, or chewing on one side can signal dental pain and need a vet visit.
