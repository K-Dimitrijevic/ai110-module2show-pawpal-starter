# Adult Cat Daily Care

## Feeding cats
Adult cats do well on two or more measured meals a day, or several small meals. Cats prefer fresh water away from their food bowl; a water fountain can encourage drinking.

## Play and enrichment
Indoor cats need at least two play sessions a day of 10 to 15 minutes, using wand toys that imitate prey. Puzzle feeders and window perches add mental stimulation.

## Litter box care
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and fully change the litter weekly. A 5 minute daily scooping task keeps cats using the box.

## Grooming cats
Short-haired cats benefit from weekly brushing; long-haired cats need brushing several times a week to prevent mats and hairballs.
