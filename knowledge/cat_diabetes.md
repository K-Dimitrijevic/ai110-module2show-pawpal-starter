# Diabetes in Cats

## Insulin and meal timing
Diabetic cats usually receive insulin twice a day, about 12 hours apart, given with or right after a meal. Insulin should be a required task and must not be skipped or doubled without vet advice.

## Diet for diabetic cats
A consistent, often low-carbohydrate wet food diet is usually recommended. Feed the same amount at the same times every day.

## Monitoring
Track water intake, urination, and appetite daily. Some owners check blood glucose at home on a schedule set by their vet.
