# Adult Dog Daily Care

## Daily exercise
Most healthy adult dogs need 30 to 120 minutes of activity a day depending on breed and energy level. Split exercise into at least two walks, morning and evening. High-energy working breeds benefit from an extra play or training session.

## Feeding schedule
Feed adult dogs twice a day at consistent times, roughly 8 to 12 hours apart. Measure portions by the food label and the dog's body condition. Fresh water should always be available.

## Mental enrichment
Dogs need daily mental stimulation as well as physical exercise. A 10 to 15 minute session of training, a puzzle feeder, or a sniff walk reduces boredom and destructive behavior.

## Grooming
Brush short-coated dogs weekly and long-coated dogs several times a week. Check ears and trim nails roughly every 3 to 4 weeks.
