# Senior Cat Care

## When a cat is senior
Cats are generally considered senior from about 10 to 11 years old. Senior cats should see a vet every 6 months.

## Gentle play for senior cats
Older cats still need daily activity. Offer two short, gentle play sessions of 5 to 10 minutes rather than long vigorous ones.

## Grooming help for senior cats
Senior cats often groom themselves less well, especially if they are stiff. Gentle brushing 2 to 3 times a week, or daily for long-haired cats, keeps the coat healthy.

## Comfort and hydration
Provide easy-access litter boxes with low sides and extra water sources. Monitor appetite and weight weekly, since weight loss can be an early sign of illness.
