# Hyperthyroidism in Cats

## Thyroid medication
Cats with hyperthyroidism are commonly treated with daily medication, often given once or twice a day at consistent times. Thyroid medication should be treated as a required task. Giving it with a meal or a small treat can make dosing easier.

## Weight and appetite monitoring
Hyperthyroid cats often lose weight despite a big appetite. Weigh the cat weekly and note any changes in eating, vomiting, or drinking.

## Vet follow-up
Thyroid levels need regular blood tests, usually every few months once the dose is stable.
